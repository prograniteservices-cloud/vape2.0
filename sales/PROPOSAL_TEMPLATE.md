## Current Situation

Vape & More currently operates their online storefront through a Clover subdomain (vape-more.cloveronline.com) with the following limitations:

- **No Google indexing** — The store does not appear in search results, making it invisible to new customers
- **No custom domain** — Lacks professional brand identity (appears as a subdomain of cloveronline.com)
- **6,817+ products with no navigation** — Customers cannot efficiently find what they need
- **No product images** — Items are listed as text only
- **Poor page speed** — Slow loading causes customer abandonment
- **No mobile optimization** — Unusable on smartphones where 60%+ of shoppers browse

# Demo Script — VapeOS 2.0

**Live Demo:** https://vape-shop-mvp-2026.vercel.app

---

## Before the Demo (2 min setup)

1. Open the live site on your laptop
2. Also open it on your phone (show mobile view)
3. Have PageSpeed Insights ready: https://pagespeed.web.dev/

---

## The Pitch (3-5 minutes)

### Step 1: Show the Problem (30 seconds)

> "Right now your store is on a Clover subdomain. It doesn't show up on Google, customers can't find anything among your 6,800+ products, and there are no images. Let me show you what I built to fix all of that."

### Step 2: Show the Homepage (30 seconds)

Open the live site. Point out:
- Clean, professional design
- "VapeOS v2" branding (this is YOUR store)
- The AI Voice Bot in the center

> "This is your new storefront. Custom domain, your branding, loads in under 2 seconds."

### Step 3: Show the Navigation (1 minute)

Click through categories in the sidebar:
- Click "Vapes" → shows 877 products
- Click "Cigarettes" → shows 232 products
- Click "Cigars" → shows 160 products
- Show how categories expand with sub-items

> "Every one of your 6,800+ products is organized into categories. Customers find what they want in seconds, not minutes."

### Step 4: Show the AI Chatbot (1 minute)

Click the mic button or type a message:
- Type: "Show me watermelon vapes"
- Show the AI responding with product recommendations
- If voice works, speak into the mic

> "This is the AI assistant. Customers can type or talk to find products. It understands natural language — 'Show me Elf Bar,' 'What's on sale?' — and navigates them to the right products automatically."

### Step 5: Show Mobile (30 seconds)

Open the site on your phone or use browser dev tools:
- Show the mobile hamburger menu
- Show the tap-friendly category navigation
- Show the PWA install prompt (if available)

> "It works perfectly on phones. Customers can even install it like an app on their home screen."

### Step 6: Show Performance (30 seconds)

Run PageSpeed Insights on the live URL:
- Show the score (should be 80+)
- Compare to their current site (which scores poorly)

> "Your current site scores poorly on Google's speed test. This one loads in under 2 seconds. Google rewards fast sites with better search rankings."

### Step 7: Close (30 seconds)

> "This is everything — custom design, AI chatbot, mobile app, SEO optimization, fast loading. The investment is $[1,500/2,500/4,000] one-time. No monthly software fees. I can have this live on your domain in 2-3 weeks."
